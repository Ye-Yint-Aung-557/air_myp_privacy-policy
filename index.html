Privacy Policy

Service: Mann Yadanarpon Airlines (MYP) Mobile App & Website (https://airmyp.com/)
Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Mann Yadanarpon Airlines ("we," "our," or "us") operates the https://airmyp.com/ website and the Mann Yadanarpon Airlines (MYP) mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our mobile application or book flights through https://airmyp.com/, we may collect certain personally identifiable information necessary to provide airline booking and customer support services, including:

  • Passenger full name, title, date of birth, and nationality/passport or NRC details (required for domestic/international flight ticketing).
  • Contact details such as email address and phone number for booking confirmations and flight schedule updates.
  • Flight itinerary, booking reference (PNR), and payment transaction references processed securely through authorized payment gateways.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To process, confirm, and manage your flight reservations and ticket issuances.
  • To send important flight alerts, schedule changes, and promotional updates via push notifications, SMS, or email.
  • To provide customer support through our branch offices and online channels.
  • To improve app stability, security, and overall user experience.

3. Third-Party Services & Web Portal

Our mobile application integrates directly with our official web portal (https://airmyp.com/) and utilizes trusted third-party services to function properly:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android functionality and app distribution.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to deliver push notifications regarding flight updates and announcements. FCM may collect device identifiers (such as Firebase installation IDs) solely for message delivery.

4. Log Data & Cookies

In the event of an app error or when browsing https://airmyp.com/, standard diagnostic data ("Log Data") and session cookies may be collected. This may include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model, operating system version, browser type, and timestamps to ensure secure session handling during flight searches and bookings.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures (including HTTPS encryption) to protect your personal and booking data against unauthorized access. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill your travel bookings and comply with aviation and legal regulations.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for independent use by children under the age of 13. Passenger information for minors traveling with adults is collected strictly for flight ticketing purposes with parental or guardian consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
